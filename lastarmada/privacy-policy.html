Privacy Policy

Last Armada: Endworld (Android) · Effective date: November 23, 2026

The short version
  • The game is played offline against computer-controlled fleets. Your progress (salvage coins, unlocked nations, best scores) and settings are stored only on your device. We have no server, no account and no sign-up.
  • The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob between matches when the device is online. Google may collect device information such as your advertising ID to show and measure ads.
  • Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time with the Privacy options button on the main menu.

1. Who we are

This policy applies to the Last Armada: Endworld game for Android (package com.anhrice.lastarmada), published by anhrice (“we”, “us”). If you have any question about this policy, contact us at tanh.naht@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device

To keep your progress, the game saves in its private storage on your device: your salvage coins, the nations you have unlocked and the one you command, your best score for each mode and difficulty, the number of matches played, fleets sunk and Doomsday wins, your chosen mode and difficulty, and your sound and vibration settings.

To offer your home nation for free, the game reads your device’s language and region setting (for example “en-US”) on the device. This setting is not stored or sent anywhere.

These values are not sent to us or to any third party, and they are not used for advertising. If Android’s own device backup is turned on, Android may include them in your Google account backup; that backup is handled by Google under its own terms, and we cannot access it.

The rival fleets in the game are computer-controlled. There is no chat, no multiplayer and no way to share content with other users. Flags and nation names are used for fun only and carry no political meaning.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Last Armada: Endworld shows full-screen ads at natural breaks (after a match ends, at most every few matches), provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. You can also choose to watch an ad to unlock a nation for free or to continue a match after your fleet sinks; such ads only play when you tap that button. Ads never appear during a match and only after your consent choice (where required) has been made. Ads are limited to content rated PG or lower. To serve ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the game may automatically collect and share with Google:

DataPurpose
Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app-set IDAdvertising, analytics, fraud prevention
IP address and approximate location derived from itAdvertising, fraud prevention, legal compliance
Device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version)Ad delivery and diagnostics

This data is collected by Google, not by us, and is encrypted in transit. Google’s use of it is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. On supported Android versions, the SDK may also use Android’s Privacy Sandbox APIs (Topics and Attribution).

4. Your choices about ads

  • Consent. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or another region where consent is required, the game shows Google’s consent message the first time you open it. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time with Privacy options on the main menu.
  • US state privacy rights. Where applicable, the same screen lets you opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information for targeted advertising.
  • Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device’s settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads on most Android devices).
  • If you decline personalised ads, you may still see non-personalised ads. Playing with the device offline shows no ads at all.

5. Permissions

The game does not ask for access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files. The permissions it declares (internet access, network state and advertising ID) are used only so that ads can be loaded. Short vibrations (which you can turn off on the main menu) use Android’s standard touch feedback and need no permission.

6. Data retention and deletion

Your progress and settings stay on your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes everything. We hold no copy and therefore cannot recover it. For data collected by Google for advertising, see Google’s policy linked above and My Ad Center.

7. Children

Last Armada: Endworld is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or US state privacy laws), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. As we do not store any of your data, most of these rights can be exercised directly in the game or in your device settings; requests about advertising data can be made to Google. You can also contact us at the address above and we will help.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the game gains new features. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If a change is significant, we will also mention it in the game’s release notes.
