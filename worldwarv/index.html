WORLD WAR V

TACTICAL GRID

The year is 2145. Twelve warlords fight over the ruins of the Earth. Take command, swap modules on the Tactical Grid and out-think every enemy in turn-based match-3 battles, all offline.

Match to fight

Line up Warheads to strike, Nano-bots to repair, Plasma Shields to defend and Quantum Cores to charge your superweapons.

Tank warfare

Modern battle tanks on both ends of the grid trade cannon fire with every match you make.

Turn-based duels

You and the enemy commander share one grid, 15 seconds per turn. Match 4 for an armour-piercing strike and an extra turn.

💣

System Overload

Match 5 or a T / L shape to drop a bomb on the grid, and chain falling modules into massive combos.

12 superweapons

Railgun, EMP Pulse, Cyber Virus, Orbital Laser, Nuclear Strike… capture each warlord’s capital to seize their weapon.

🗺️

36-mission campaign

Fight across the Ash Wastes, the Frozen North and High Orbit to Ground Zero, then keep going in Endless War.

🔧

Upgrade and rise

Spend black credits in the workshop on hull, firepower, armour and electronic warfare, and earn promotions with intel.
