Privacy Policy

Flag Quiz Rush: World Flags (Android) · Effective date: November 26, 2026

The short version
  • Your game progress (stars, coins, lantern colours, daily streak) and settings are stored only on your device. We have no server and never receive them. There is no account, no chat and no sign-up.
  • The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. Because children may play it, every ad request is marked for child-directed treatment: ads are not personalised and are limited to content rated G (suitable for all ages).
  • Where the law requires it, Google's consent message is shown before ads, and you can change your choice later with Privacy options in Settings.

1. Who we are

This policy applies to the Flag Quiz Rush: World Flags game for Android (package com.anhrice.flagquizrush), published by anhrice ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy, contact us at tanh.naht@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device

To work, the game saves in its private storage on your device: the stars you earned in each World Tour stop, your coins, the flags collected in your Atlas, your best scores, your Daily Quiz streak, trophy progress, whether you have seen the tutorial, and your sound, music and vibration settings.

These values are not sent to us or to any third party, and they are not used for advertising. If Android's own device backup is turned on, Android may include them in your Google account backup; that backup is handled by Google under its own terms, and we cannot access it.

The game has no account, no chat, no way to contact other players and no way to share content. Coins are earned only by playing; there are no in-app purchases.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows occasional full-screen (interstitial) ads when you leave the end-of-quiz screen, never during play, and optional rewarded videos that you can choose to watch to double your coins or continue a Survival or Time Rush run. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC, which takes part in Google Play's Families Ads Program.

Every ad request from the game is sent with child-directed (under the age of consent) treatment and a maximum ad content rating of G. With these settings Google does not show personalised (interest-based) ads or remarketing, and limits the data it uses. To deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may still process:

DataPurpose
IP address and approximate location derived from itAd delivery (contextual), fraud prevention, legal compliance
Device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version)Ad delivery and diagnostics
Interactions with ads (views, taps) and crash/performance logs of the ad SDKAd measurement, fraud prevention, diagnostics

This data is collected by Google, not by us, and is encrypted in transit. Google's use of it is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Your choices about ads

  • Consent. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or another region where consent is required, the game shows Google's consent message the first time you open it. You can change or withdraw your choice with Privacy options in Settings (the gear icon on the main menu).
  • Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads on most Android devices).
  • Rewarded videos are optional. You never have to watch one to play every mode.

5. Children

Flag Quiz Rush is a family game that children may enjoy. We do not ask for, collect or store any personal information from anyone, including children: there is no sign-up, no name or age field, no chat and no links that leave the game except this policy and the consent form. Ads are configured as described in section 3. If you are a parent and have any concern, contact us at the address above.

6. Permissions

The game does not ask for access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files. The permissions it declares (internet access and network state) are used only so that ads can be loaded.

7. Data retention and deletion

Your progress and settings stay on your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes everything. We hold no copy and therefore cannot recover it. For data processed by Google for advertising, see Google's policy linked above.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, COPPA or US state privacy laws), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of personal data, and to withdraw consent. As we do not store any of your data, most of these rights can be exercised directly in the game or in your device settings; requests about advertising data can be made to Google. You can also contact us and we will help.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the game gains new features. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If a change is significant, we will also mention it in the game's release notes.
