Privacy Policy

Block Siege: Castle Crush (Android) · Effective date: November 24, 2026

The short version
  • The game is played offline. Your progress (gold, siege level, castles destroyed, Classic best score) and settings are stored only on your device. We have no server, no account and no sign-up.
  • The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob between rounds when the device is online. Google may collect device information such as your advertising ID to show and measure ads.
  • Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time with Privacy options on the game’s main menu.

1. Who we are

This policy applies to the Block Siege: Castle Crush game for Android (package com.anhrice.blocksiege), published by anhrice (“we”, “us”). If you have any question about this policy, contact us at tanh.naht@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device

To keep your progress, the game saves in its private storage on your device: your gold, your current siege level, the number of castles you have destroyed, your best Classic score, whether you have seen the tutorial, and your sound, music and vibration settings.

These values are not sent to us or to any third party, and they are not used for advertising. If Android’s own device backup is turned on, Android may include them in your Google account backup; that backup is handled by Google under its own terms, and we cannot access it.

The castles in the game are computer-controlled. There is no chat, no multiplayer and no way to share content with other users.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Block Siege shows full-screen ads at natural breaks (when you leave a game-over screen, at most every few rounds and never in the first minute after launch), provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. You can also choose to watch an ad to continue a lost game or to double a reward; such ads only play when you tap that button. Ads never appear during a round and only after your consent choice (where required) has been made. Ads are limited to content rated PG or lower. To serve ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the game may automatically collect and share with Google:

DataPurpose

This data is collected by Google, not by us, and is encrypted in transit. Google’s use of it is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. On supported Android versions, the SDK may also use Android’s Privacy Sandbox APIs (Topics and Attribution).

4. Your choices about ads

  • Consent. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or another region where consent is required, the game shows Google’s consent message the first time you open it. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time with Privacy options on the main menu.
  • US state privacy rights. Where applicable, the same screen lets you opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information for targeted advertising.
  • Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device’s settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads on most Android devices).
  • If you decline personalised ads, you may still see non-personalised ads. Playing with the device offline shows no ads at all.

5. Permissions

The game does not ask for access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files. The permissions it declares (internet access, network state and advertising ID, added by the ads SDK) are used only so that ads can be loaded. Short vibrations (which you can turn off in the game) use Android’s standard touch feedback and need no permission.

6. Data retention and deletion

Your progress and settings stay on your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes everything. We hold no copy and therefore cannot recover it. For data collected by Google for advertising, see Google’s policy linked above and My Ad Center.

7. Children

Block Siege is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or US state privacy laws), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. As we do not store any of your data, most of these rights can be exercised directly in the game or in your device settings; requests about advertising data can be made to Google. You can also contact us at the address above and we will help.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the game gains new features. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If a change is significant, we will also mention it in the game’s release notes.
