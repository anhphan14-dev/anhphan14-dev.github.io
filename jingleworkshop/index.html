Jingle Workshop: Gift Match

Santa's elves caught the sniffles, and the children are lining up with their wishes. Match the materials, craft every gift and keep the line moving all the way to Christmas Eve!

Get it on Google Play
🎁

Every child has a wish

A teddy bear, a wooden train, a space rocket... Each wish card shows the wood, yarn, candy, stars, gears and magic it needs.

🍬

Real-time match 3

Line up 3 or more and the materials fly to the children who need them. 4 in a row doubles them; 5, T or L sets off a Jingle Blast.

⏳

Three counters, one line

Serve three children at once while others wait in the snow. Too slow, or a line that overflows, and a heart is lost.

📅

24 Advent nights

Open a calendar door every night from December 1 to 24, unlock 16 toys, earn up to 3 stars, then try Endless Eve.

☕

Santa's powers

Fill the Cheer meter to pour Hot Cocoa, call a Snow Freeze, send the Reindeer Dash or let an Elf Helper finish a wish.

🎄

Cosy and offline

Hand-drawn Christmas art, sleigh bells and carols. Upgrade the workshop with coins. Plays without internet.
