Privacy Policy

Life in Weeks (Android) · Effective date: November 19, 2026

The short version
  • Everything you write in Life in Weeks (your date of birth, notes, milestones, life stages and loved ones) is stored only on your device. We do not have a server and never receive it.
  • The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device information such as your advertising ID to show and measure ads.
  • Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.

1. Who we are

This policy applies to the Life in Weeks app for Android (package com.anhrice.lifeinweeks), published by anhrice (“we”, “us”). If you have any question about this policy, contact us at tanh.naht@gmail.com.

2. Information you enter in the app

To draw your life as a grid of weeks, the app lets you enter:

  • your date of birth and the lifespan you choose;
  • names and starting ages of your life stages;
  • notes and milestones attached to particular weeks;
  • loved ones you add: a name, relationship, birth year and how often you see them.

This information is saved in the app’s private storage on your device. It is not sent to us or to any third party, and it is not used for advertising. The home-screen widget reads your date of birth and lifespan from the same on-device storage.

Backups. If you use Back up to file, the app writes your data to a file in a location you choose; what happens to that file afterwards (for example, if you save it to a cloud drive) is under your control. If Android’s own device backup is turned on, Android may include the app’s data in your Google account backup; that backup is handled by Google under its own terms, and we cannot access it.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Life in Weeks shows full-screen (interstitial) ads provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To serve ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the app may automatically collect and share with Google:

DataPurpose
Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app-set IDAdvertising, analytics, fraud prevention
IP address and approximate location derived from itAdvertising, fraud prevention, legal compliance
Device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version)Ad delivery and diagnostics
Interactions with ads (views, taps) and crash/performance logs of the ad SDKAd measurement, analytics, diagnostics

This data is collected by Google, not by us, and is encrypted in transit. Google’s use of it is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. On supported Android versions, the SDK may also use Android’s Privacy Sandbox APIs (Topics and Attribution).

4. Your choices about ads

  • Consent. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or another region where consent is required, the app shows Google’s consent message the first time you open it. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • US state privacy rights. Where applicable, the same screen lets you opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information for targeted advertising.
  • Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device’s settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads on most Android devices).
  • If you decline personalised ads, you may still see non-personalised ads, which are based on context rather than your profile.

5. Permissions

The app does not ask for access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files. The permissions it declares (internet access, network state and advertising ID) are used only so that ads can be loaded.

6. Data retention and deletion

Because the information you enter stays on your device, you are in full control of it: editing or deleting it in the app removes it, and uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes all of it. We hold no copy and therefore cannot recover it. For data collected by Google for advertising, see Google’s policy linked above and My Ad Center.

7. Children

Life in Weeks is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or US state privacy laws), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. As we do not store any of your data, most of these rights can be exercised directly in the app or in your device settings; requests about advertising data can be made to Google. You can also contact us at the address above and we will help.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the app gains new features. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If a change is significant, we will also mention it in the app’s release notes.
