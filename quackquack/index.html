DUCKLING BATTLE ROYALE

Ducklings hatch and follow the first thing they see: a Mama Duck, a kid, a puppy, a bicycle, even a red car. Lead the longest line, bite rival lines to steal their ducklings, and be the last leader standing. Fully offline.

Get it on Google Play
🥚

Hatch and lead

Run over eggs: each duckling imprints on you and joins your line. Golden eggs hatch three at once.

🦆

Bite to steal

Touch a rival's line with your head and every duckling behind the bite follows you instead.

🛡️

Ducklings are your shield

While you have ducklings you can't be knocked out. Lose them all and any leader can catch you!

🏆

Battle royale

The pond dries up step by step. On the mud your ducklings wander off. Last leader standing wins.

🚲

9 leaders

Mama Duck, Little Kid, Puppy, Bicycle, Grumpy Goose, Scooter, Red Car, Tractor and Robot Vacuum, each with its own speed, turning and bite.

📱

Portrait or landscape

Drag to steer and hold DASH, or turn your phone: left thumb steers, right thumb dashes.

✈️

Fully offline

No account and no internet needed. Bread, umbrella and sneakers power-ups keep every match different.
