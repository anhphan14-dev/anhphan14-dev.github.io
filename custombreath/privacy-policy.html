Privacy Policy – Custom Breath

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Custom Breath ("the app") is a guided breathing and meditation timer for Android. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the following only on your device. It is never uploaded to us or to anyone else:

  • Your breathing settings (pattern, phase durations, session length, voice and music switches).
  • Your practice statistics (minutes practised per day and number of sessions).
  • Your reminder settings (reminder times, time window and days of the week).

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes this information. We do not operate any server and do not create user accounts.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows banner ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID.
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address.
  • Information about ad interactions, app performance and crash diagnostics.

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us. Data is encrypted in transit. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time using Privacy settings at the bottom of the main screen. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads).

Notifications and alarms

If you turn on reminders, the app asks for permission to show notifications and may ask you to allow "Alarms & reminders" so reminders arrive on time. These permissions are used only to show the breathing reminders you scheduled.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. The new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
