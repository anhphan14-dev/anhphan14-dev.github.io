Privacy Policy

Snap Pair (Android) · Effective date: November 21, 2026

The short version
  • Your records, collected stars and settings are stored only on your device. We do not have a server and never receive them. There is no account and no sign-up.
  • The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device information such as your advertising ID to show and measure ads.
  • Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time with the Ad privacy choices button on the game’s main menu.

1. Who we are

This policy applies to the Snap Pair game for Android (package com.anhrice.snappair), published by anhrice (“we”, “us”). If you have any question about this policy, contact us at tanh.naht@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves a few values in its private storage on your device: your records for each game mode and difficulty (best score, best time, wins against the bot), the total number of stars you have collected, the number of games played, the difficulty you last chose, whether sound is on or off, and whether you have seen the “How to play” screen.

These values are not sent to us or to any third party, and they are not used for advertising. If Android’s own device backup is turned on, Android may include them in your Google account backup; that backup is handled by Google under its own terms, and we cannot access it.

The 2-player mode is played on a single device. The game has no chat, no online multiplayer and no way to share content with other users.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Snap Pair shows full-screen (interstitial) ads provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC, only when you leave the results screen between games (never during a game). Ads are limited to content rated PG or lower. To serve ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the game may automatically collect and share with Google:

DataPurpose
Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app-set IDAdvertising, analytics, fraud prevention
IP address and approximate location derived from itAdvertising, fraud prevention, legal compliance
Device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version)Ad delivery and diagnostics
Interactions with ads (views, taps) and crash/performance logs of the ad SDKAd measurement, analytics, diagnostics

This data is collected by Google, not by us, and is encrypted in transit. Google’s use of it is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. On supported Android versions, the SDK may also use Android’s Privacy Sandbox APIs (Topics and Attribution).

4. Your choices about ads

  • Consent. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or another region where consent is required, the game shows Google’s consent message the first time you open it. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time with the Ad privacy choices button on the main menu.
  • US state privacy rights. Where applicable, the same screen lets you opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information for targeted advertising.
  • Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device’s settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads on most Android devices).
  • If you decline personalised ads, you may still see non-personalised ads, which are based on context rather than your profile.

5. Permissions

The game does not ask for access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files. The permissions it declares (internet access, network state and advertising ID) are used only so that ads can be loaded. Short vibrations when you tap use Android’s standard touch feedback and need no permission.

6. Data retention and deletion

Your records and settings stay on your device: uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes all of them. We hold no copy and therefore cannot recover them. For data collected by Google for advertising, see Google’s policy linked above and My Ad Center.

7. Children

Snap Pair is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or US state privacy laws), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. As we do not store any of your data, most of these rights can be exercised directly in the game or in your device settings; requests about advertising data can be made to Google. You can also contact us at the address above and we will help.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the game gains new features such as online leaderboards. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If a change is significant, we will also mention it in the game’s release notes.
