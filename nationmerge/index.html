NATION MERGE

NAVAL WAR

Buy warships, merge two identical ships into a stronger one and watch your fleet battle the navies of the world. Win battles, conquer nations, all offline.

Get it on Google Play
🔀

Merge to grow

Drag a ship onto an identical one: two Patrol Boats become a Gunboat, two Gunboats a Corvette, all the way to the Leviathan.

⚓

24 warships

Two lines of 12 tiers: tough cannon warships for the front line, long-range missile ships with splash damage behind.

💥

Auto battles

Tap FIGHT and your fleet sails into battle on its own: shells, missiles, explosions and sinking ships.

🚩

Conquer 48 nations

Every battle is against another nation’s navy. Defeat a fleet and you can command that nation yourself.

🧠

Plan your formation

Place your docks wisely and check the power meter before you sail. Every 10th battle is a boss flagship.

✈️

Fully offline

No account and no internet needed to play. A naval soundtrack, a shipyard codex and short battles you can play anywhere.
