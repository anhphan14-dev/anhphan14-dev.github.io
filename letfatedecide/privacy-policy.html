Privacy Policy

Let Fate Decide (Android) · Effective date: November 22, 2026

The short version
  • Your wheels, lists, decision history and settings are stored only on your device. We do not have a server and never receive them. There is no account and no sign-up.
  • The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device information such as your advertising ID to show and measure ads.
  • Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time with Privacy options in the app’s Settings (the sliders icon on the home screen).

1. Who we are

This policy applies to the Let Fate Decide app for Android (package com.anhrice.letfatedecide), published by anhrice (“we”, “us”). If you have any question about this policy, contact us at tanh.naht@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device

To work, the app saves a few things in its private storage on your device: the wheels and option lists you create, your card options, the two choices you last typed in “This or That”, your own food list, coin-flip counts and side names, the history of your recent decisions (up to 200 entries, including any item name you typed in “Should I Buy It?”), and your sound and vibration settings.

These values are not sent to us or to any third party, and they are not used for advertising. If Android’s own device backup is turned on, Android may include them in your Google account backup; that backup is handled by Google under its own terms, and we cannot access it.

The app has no account, no chat and no way to share content with other users. Its decisions are random and made for fun; they are not financial or professional advice.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Let Fate Decide shows full-screen (interstitial) ads provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC, only when you go back from a tool to the home screen after making some decisions (never while a result is on screen). Ads are limited to content rated PG or lower. To serve ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the app may automatically collect and share with Google:

DataPurpose
Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app-set IDAdvertising, analytics, fraud prevention
IP address and approximate location derived from itAdvertising, fraud prevention, legal compliance
Device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version)Ad delivery and diagnostics
Interactions with ads (views, taps) and crash/performance logs of the ad SDKAd measurement, analytics, diagnostics

This data is collected by Google, not by us, and is encrypted in transit. Google’s use of it is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. On supported Android versions, the SDK may also use Android’s Privacy Sandbox APIs (Topics and Attribution).

4. Your choices about ads

  • Consent. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or another region where consent is required, the app shows Google’s consent message the first time you open it. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time with Privacy options in the app’s Settings.
  • US state privacy rights. Where applicable, the same screen lets you opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information for targeted advertising.
  • Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device’s settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads on most Android devices).
  • If you decline personalised ads, you may still see non-personalised ads, which are based on context rather than your profile.

5. Permissions

The app does not ask for access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files. The permissions it declares (internet access, network state and advertising ID) are used only so that ads can be loaded. Short vibrations (which you can turn off in Settings) use Android’s standard touch feedback and need no permission.

6. Data retention and deletion

Your lists, history and settings stay on your device. You can clear the decision history in the app at any time; uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes everything. We hold no copy and therefore cannot recover it. For data collected by Google for advertising, see Google’s policy linked above and My Ad Center.

7. Children

Let Fate Decide is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or US state privacy laws), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. As we do not store any of your data, most of these rights can be exercised directly in the app or in your device settings; requests about advertising data can be made to Google. You can also contact us at the address above and we will help.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the app gains new features. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If a change is significant, we will also mention it in the app’s release notes.
