A calm, clever sort puzzle at the docks. Lift containers with the crane, stack four of a colour and watch them load onto the cargo ship. Sail from Hai Phong to Sydney, all offline.

🏗️

Tap, lift, drop

Tap a stack to lift its top containers, tap another to drop them on the same colour or an empty spot.

🚢

Load the ship

Four of one colour in a stack load straight onto the cargo ship. Fill every bay and the ship sails away.

🌏

15 famous ports

Hai Phong, Singapore, Rotterdam, New York and more. Every 10 levels a new harbor and new sky.

❓

Covered cargo

Later levels hide containers under tarps: you only see the colour once it reaches the top.

📅

Daily Cargo

A fresh puzzle every day with bonus coins. Keep your daily streak going.

✈️

Fully offline

No account, no timer and no internet needed. Undo, hints and an extra stack when you get stuck.
