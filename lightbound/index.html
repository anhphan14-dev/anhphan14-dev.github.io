Lightbound: Dark Maze

The maze is pitch black. All you have is Lumi, a little spirit of light. Find the keys, unlock the door and escape before whatever hides in the dark finds you.

Your light moves with you

You only see a few steps around you. The paths you walk stay on your map, so explore smart.

Flashlight and batteries

Shine far down a corridor, but the battery drains fast. Grab spares before the dark wins.

🧠

Memory maze

In the Fading Woods the map fades behind you. Remember the way, or light braziers to keep an area bright.

👀

Monsters in the dark

Wisps drift, Hunters chase whoever they catch in the beam, Shades creep closer when you look away. A red glow and your heartbeat warn you.

🏮

Light sources

Collect lanterns to grow your light. In the Abyss you can only see the tiles right next to you.

📅

60 mazes and a Daily Maze

5 chapters, 3 stars per maze, a fresh maze every day, 6 lantern colours. Swipe or D-pad, plays offline.
