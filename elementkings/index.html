Element Kings: Match Battle

Two Kings, two armies, one road. Match elements to summon chess soldiers and send them marching to topple the enemy King before your own tower falls!

Get it on Google Play
💧

Six elements

Water, fire, metal, wood, earth and magic fill your board. Every match summons a soldier of that element.

♟️

A chess army

Match 3 for a Pawn, 4 for a sturdy Rook, an L or T for a Knight that explodes on contact, 5 for a mighty Queen. A COMBO ×3 adds a healing Bishop.

🔥

Elements counter each other

Water beats Fire, Fire beats Metal, Metal beats Wood, Wood beats Earth, Earth beats Water. Strong hits deal 60% more.

👑

Real-time battles

The enemy King plays its own board at the same time. Both Kings shoot weakly, but matches fill Royal Energy for Arrow Rain, Royal Shield and Rally.

⏳

Tactics mode

Prefer to think? Take turns with the enemy King on one shared board, 10 seconds per move, while both armies keep fighting. Match 4, match 5 or an L/T shape to earn an extra move. Its own 30-battle campaign.

🏰

Guard towers

Each King starts behind a weak Pawn tower and one random Knight, Rook, Bishop or Queen tower. Break through them first!

🏆

Endless Arena and daily quests

Arena rounds get harder and harder while your King's health carries over. The best runs go on the Hall of Fame. Three new quests every day for bonus coins.

⚔️

Duel: vs AI or 2 players

Duel the AI Red King in real time (Easy, Normal or Hard, then unlock INSANE) while it plays its own board above yours, or challenge a friend: take turns on one shared board, or lay the phone or tablet flat for a real-time split-screen duel with one board each. An even fight with no upgrades, just skill.

🗺️

30 battles, 6 realms

From Earth Valley to the Magic Castle. Each realm favours its element, so pick the right counters.

⬆️

Upgrade and play offline

Earn coins and stars, upgrade every piece and your King. Cute art for kids and grown-ups, no internet needed.
